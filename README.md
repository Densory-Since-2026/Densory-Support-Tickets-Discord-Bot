> Support, applications, reports and orders in one bot — channel instantly, questions by click, your team sees everything on one card.

Members pick a category in the panel, the bot instantly creates a private channel and walks them through the questions you define in the dashboard — buttons, select menus, forms, even file uploads. Afterwards everything is laid out for your team: claim, set priority, add members, close with transcript and feedback. Seven industry presets (gaming, agency & trades, shop, education, events, SaaS) get you started in minutes; every category is adjustable down to the last detail, including timed application tests and confidential reports. The bot speaks 29 languages and detects each member's language automatically.

## Features

- **Instant channel** — One click in the panel and the channel exists — the rest runs on buttons and forms.
- **Any industry** — Seven presets, unlimited custom categories, questions and flows.
- **AI when you want it** — Summary, reply suggestion, translation — opt-in, redacted, never published automatically.

## Prerequisites

- Your own Discord application with a bot token (enable the Message Content intent in the Developer Portal)
- Bot permissions: Manage Channels, Manage Roles, Send Messages
- A channel category for tickets and optionally a log channel

## Setup

1. **Connect the bot**
   Store the token in the dashboard, pick the server, invite the bot.
2. **Choose a preset**
   Load an industry template in the dashboard or via /ticket setup preset and adjust the categories.
3. **Post the panel**
   /panel post in your support channel — buttons or select menu, icons of your choice.
4. **Set up your team**
   Staff roles, log channel, inactivity and transcript settings.
5. **Go**
   Claim, handle and close tickets — each with transcript, feedback and statistics.
